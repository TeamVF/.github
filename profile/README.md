# TeamVF

TeamVF builds **VisionFlow** — a local-first personal media library for photographers and filmmakers with large archives.

VisionFlow is Mac-first (with a light web companion) for people sitting on multi-terabyte photo and video collections. Phase 1 focuses on reliable multi-device sync and backup, AI understanding of people and places, natural-language search, precise clip finding, and lightweight highlight / rough-cut workflows.

We're building in private while we validate that AI can meaningfully cut the time spent managing, searching, and reviewing video. Not open for public signup yet.
